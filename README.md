# Horde Shift — Privacy Policy / Gizlilik Politikası

_Last updated: 2026-11-22_

## English
Horde Shift ("the game") is developed by Artifact Soft.

**Data we collect:** The game itself does not require an account and does not collect your name, address, or phone number. Your gameplay progress (gold, unlocked chapters, gear, achievements, settings) is stored locally on your device and optionally synced using your platform's built-in cloud backup (Google Play Auto Backup / Google Play Games on Android, Apple iCloud Key-Value Store on iOS).

**Advertising:** The game shows optional rewarded video ads and occasional interstitial ads through Unity LevelPlay (ironSource, Unity Ads, and mediated partners). These services may collect device advertising identifiers (AAID/IDFA), IP address, and ad interaction data to show and measure ads. Where required by law (GDPR/KVKK/ATT), your consent is requested before personalized ads are shown. You can reset or limit ad tracking in your device settings. Unity's privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

**In-app purchases:** Purchases are processed securely by Google Play or Apple App Store. We never receive or store your credit card or payment information.

**Children:** The game is intended for a general audience (13+) and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

**Contact:** artifactsoft0@gmail.com
